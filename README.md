## 🚀 Project Overview

**LandSure AI** is an AI-powered land intelligence and due diligence platform designed to help individuals explore, understand, and evaluate land-related information before making property decisions in Bangladesh.

Land transactions can involve complex ownership histories, multiple survey records, mutation documents, land tax information, inheritance claims, boundary issues, and legal uncertainties.

LandSure AI brings these different aspects into a unified digital experience.

The platform combines:

- Intelligent land discovery
- GIS-based parcel visualization
- Structured land record review
- Nine-step land verification
- AI-assisted risk assessment
- Ownership history visualization
- Document scanning and OCR
- AI-powered land assistance
- Registration cost estimation
- Legal consultation discovery
- Digital verification reports
- Saved properties and watchlists

Our goal is to make preliminary land due diligence more **accessible, understandable, organized, and transparent**.

> **Important:** LandSure AI is a research and hackathon prototype using synthetic demonstration data. It is not an official government land registry, legal certification service, or substitute for professional verification.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Land-related decisions in Bangladesh frequently require reviewing information from multiple records, documents, and stakeholders.

Several challenges can make this process difficult.

### 1. Fragmented Land Information

Relevant information may be spread across different survey records, ownership documents, mutation records, tax documents, and other sources.

### 2. Complex Ownership History

Understanding how ownership changed over time can be challenging, especially when records contain inconsistencies or incomplete information.

### 3. Risk of Document Inconsistencies

Differences in names, land area, parcel identifiers, and supporting documentation can create uncertainty.

### 4. Limited Accessibility of Technical Information

Many users are unfamiliar with legal and administrative terminology related to land transactions.

### 5. Time-Consuming Preliminary Review

Organizing and comparing land-related documents manually can require significant effort.

### 6. Difficulty Understanding Potential Risks

Users may struggle to identify which issues require further investigation or professional assistance.

### 7. Lack of a Unified Digital Workflow

Land discovery, document review, risk analysis, and reporting are often handled separately.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

LandSure AI introduces a unified **AI-assisted land due diligence workflow**.

Instead of navigating disconnected processes, users can explore available demonstration parcel data, review supporting information, examine potential inconsistencies, and generate structured summaries through a single platform.

### Our Approach

**Discover → Explore → Save → Verify → Analyze → Consult → Report**

The system is designed around three core principles:

**Transparency:** Present available evidence and clearly identify uncertainty.

**Accessibility:** Explain complex land information through understandable interfaces and Bengali-first user experiences.

**Decision Support:** Help users recognize issues that may require additional documentation or professional review.

LandSure AI does not replace official verification. It helps users organize and understand the information available within the prototype.

## ✨ Core Features

### 🗺️ 1. Smart Land Discovery

Explore available land parcels through an interactive discovery interface.

**Capabilities:**

- District-based parcel search
- Upazila and locality filters where data is available
- Parcel information cards
- Land area and location details
- Interactive GIS map
- Parcel selection
- Saved property workflow
- Navigation into detailed verification

The discovery experience is designed to help users move from a broad location search to an individual parcel assessment.

### 📍 2. Interactive GIS Parcel Visualization

Visualize land parcels geographically using map-based interfaces.

**Capabilities:**

- Geospatial parcel markers
- Approximate location visualization
- Parcel highlighting
- Interactive selection
- Land information overlays
- Location-based exploration

GIS data in the prototype is synthetic or approximate and must not be interpreted as legally surveyed boundaries.

### 🧾 3. Khatian and Survey Comparison

Support structured review of historical land survey information.

The verification workflow includes:

- CS — Cadastral Survey
- SA — State Acquisition Survey
- RS — Revisional Survey
- BS — Bangladesh Survey

The interface helps users understand how survey information may differ across records.

### 👥 4. Ownership History and Chain of Title

Present available historical ownership information through a structured timeline.

**Capabilities:**

- Ownership sequence visualization
- Historical transaction references
- Seller and buyer relationship review
- Supporting record comparison
- Potential inconsistency indicators

Where sufficient information is available, the system is designed to support extended ownership-history review, including a 25-year or longer period.

The system does not invent missing historical records.

### 📝 5. Mutation and Record Review

Organize mutation-related information and identify possible inconsistencies between available ownership and mutation records.

### 💰 6. Land Development Tax Review

Provide a structured interface for examining available land tax information and identifying records requiring further confirmation.

### 📐 7. Boundary, Road Access and Possession Review

Help users consider issues related to:

- Parcel boundaries
- Road accessibility
- Possession information
- Area discrepancies
- Supporting evidence

The platform does not independently establish physical possession or legally certify boundaries.

### ⚖️ 8. Court and Inheritance Review

Provide a workflow for reviewing available information relating to land disputes, succession, and inheritance.

Actual court or government record verification requires access to authorized, reliable sources.

### 🤖 9. AI-Powered Risk Assessment

Use machine-learning-assisted analysis to highlight possible inconsistencies and review requirements.

**Capabilities:**

- Structured input analysis
- Risk-related observations
- Evidence-aware assessment
- Warning indicators
- Human review recommendations

AI outputs are decision-support indicators, not legal verdicts or guarantees of fraud detection.

### 📄 10. Document OCR and Analysis

Support the digitization and review of land-related documents.

**Capabilities:**

- Document upload
- Text extraction where supported
- Structured information presentation
- Field review
- Identification of potential discrepancies
- Integration with the broader verification experience

Extracted information should be reviewed for accuracy before being relied upon.

### 💬 11. AI Land Assistant

A Bengali-oriented assistant interface designed to help users understand land-related concepts and navigate the platform.

**Example questions:**

- What is a khatian?
- Why is mutation important?
- What should I check before buying land?
- What is the difference between CS, SA, RS, and BS records?
- Which documents may be needed for a land transaction?

The assistant is intended for informational guidance, not professional legal advice.

### 📊 12. Land Trust Passport

Generate a structured digital summary of the available land verification information.

A report may include:

- Parcel overview
- Location and area
- Ownership information
- Survey record comparison
- Supporting evidence
- AI-assisted observations
- Risk indicators
- Review recommendations

The Land Trust Passport is a **prototype assessment report**, not an official ownership certificate or government-issued document.

### 🧮 13. Registration Cost Calculator

Help users estimate land transaction-related costs using available inputs and configured calculation rules.

Estimates are informational and may not reflect every applicable fee, exemption, or current legal requirement.

### 👨‍⚖️ 14. Lawyer Consultation

Provide an interface for exploring legal consultation options relevant to land-related questions.

The prototype includes synthetic demonstration lawyer profiles.

The system does not imply that these profiles represent verified practicing lawyers or confirmed appointments.

### 🔖 15. Saved Properties and Watchlist

Allow users to organize parcels they want to revisit.

**Capabilities:**

- Save selected parcels
- Review saved land information
- Return to parcel details
- Continue verification
- Access available watchlist information

Monitoring and notifications are limited to events supported by the prototype. The system does not continuously monitor external government registries.

### 🔔 16. Notifications and My Land

Provide dedicated interfaces for managing available parcel information, user activity, and supported notifications.

### 🌐 17. Bengali-First Responsive Experience

LandSure AI emphasizes accessibility for Bangladeshi users.

The interface includes:

- Bengali-oriented content
- English-language support where implemented
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Clear navigation
- Accessible interaction patterns
- Consistent dark emerald visual identity

## 🛡️ Nine-Step Land Verification

The nine-step verification workflow is one of the core features of LandSure AI.

| Step | Verification Module | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Khatian and Survey Comparison | Compare available CS, SA, RS, and BS records |
| 02 | Ownership History | Review historical ownership and transaction records |
| 03 | Mutation Verification | Examine mutation-related information |
| 04 | Land Development Tax | Review available tax records |
| 05 | Boundary and Possession | Consider boundary, access, and possession evidence |
| 06 | Court and Inheritance | Review available dispute and succession information |
| 07 | AI Risk Assessment | Identify potential inconsistencies and review needs |
| 08 | Registration Cost Calculator | Estimate relevant transaction costs |
| 09 | Change Watchlist | Organize supported monitoring and notifications |

### Verification Philosophy

The workflow separates:

- Available supporting evidence
- Potential inconsistencies
- Missing or insufficient information
- Items requiring professional review

The system is designed to avoid treating missing evidence as proof of wrongdoing.

## 🧠 AI and Machine Learning

LandSure AI incorporates machine-learning components into its backend architecture.

### 1. Parcel Review Model

**Artifact:** `logistic_regression_v2.joblib`

**Purpose:** Support structured parcel review and risk-related assessment using available features.

### 2. Feature / Query Routing Model

**Artifact:** `landsure_feature_router_v4.joblib`

**Purpose:** Help route supported user requests or inputs toward appropriate platform functionality.

### 3. Safety Gate Model

**Artifact:** `landsure_safety_gate_v2.joblib`

**Purpose:** Support safety-related handling and classification within the AI interaction workflow.

### Model Integration

The models are stored and loaded on the backend.

Frontend components communicate with backend endpoints instead of directly executing serialized Python models in the browser.

### Responsible AI Principles

- Do not treat AI output as conclusive legal evidence.
- Clearly distinguish missing information from detected inconsistencies.
- Avoid unsupported confidence claims.
- Keep human review central to important decisions.
- Do not represent synthetic training data as verified real-world records.

**Note:** Model performance metrics should be published only when supported by reproducible evaluation results.

## 🔌 Backend and API Integration

The React frontend communicates with the FastAPI backend over HTTPS.

**Production Backend:**

https://landsure-ai-backend.onrender.com

**Frontend:**

https://landsure-ai.pages.dev/

API requests support the platform's implemented services, including parcel discovery, verification, AI-related workflows, and other backend-enabled functionality.

### Production Configuration

The frontend may use a Vite environment variable such as:

```env
VITE_API_URL=https://landsure-ai-backend.onrender.com
```

The exact value must match the repository's API client implementation and base-path configuration.

Never include private API keys, passwords, or server-side credentials in variables prefixed with `VITE_`.

For the deployed backend, documentation availability depends on production configuration.

## ⚙️ Installation and Setup

### Prerequisites

Install:

- Node.js
- npm
- Python
- Git

Use compatible versions specified by the repository's dependency configuration.

### 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Shihab-10/LandSure-AI.git

cd LandSure-AI
```

### 2. Frontend Setup

```bash
cd frontend

npm install
```

Configure the frontend API URL according to the project's integration settings.

Start the development server:

```bash
npm run dev
```

The Vite development server commonly runs at:

```text
http://localhost:5173
```

### 3. Backend Setup

Open a separate terminal:

```bash
cd LandSure-AI/backend
```

Create a Python virtual environment:

```bash
python -m venv .venv
```

Activate it on Windows PowerShell:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
```

Install dependencies:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

Start the FastAPI application using the actual ASGI module configured in the repository.

For example, if the entry point is `app.main:app`:

```bash
uvicorn app.main:app --reload --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000
```

Backend development URL:

```text
http://127.0.0.1:8000
```

### 4. Local Integration

Ensure the frontend API client targets the local backend or uses the project's configured Vite development proxy.

Do not use a production backend URL unintentionally during local development.

### 5. Production Build

```bash
cd frontend

npm run build
```

The production build output is typically generated in the `dist` directory.

## ⚠️ Limitations

LandSure AI is a hackathon research prototype.

Current limitations include:

**Synthetic Data:** The dataset contains fictional demonstration records rather than certified government land records.

**Geographic Coverage:** Demonstration coverage is limited to seven districts.

**Approximate GIS Information:** Coordinates and parcel visualizations are not legal survey measurements.

**No Official Government Certification:** The platform does not issue official land ownership certificates or legal clearance.

**AI Uncertainty:** Machine-learning assessments may be incomplete or incorrect and require human interpretation.

**Document Processing Limitations:** OCR quality depends on document clarity, language, format, and processing capabilities.

**Legal Consultation:** Demonstration lawyer profiles are synthetic unless independently replaced with verified real profiles.

**Monitoring Limitations:** Watchlist functionality does not imply unrestricted access to external land registry changes.

**Integration Limitations:** Government APIs and other authoritative services require explicit authorization and reliable data access.

These limitations are intentionally disclosed to promote transparency and responsible use.
